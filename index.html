About Bethlehem

At Bethlehem Animal Clinic & Grooming, we provide veterinary and pet grooming services for pet owners in Taytay, Rizal, and nearby communities. Our goal is to help pets stay healthy, comfortable, and well cared for through attentive service and clear communication with their owners.

Our grooming services include bathing, trimming, and complete grooming packages for dogs and cats. We also offer veterinary consultations, vaccinations, treatments, surgery, diagnostic imaging, laboratory testing, and confinement services to support different pet care needs.

For added convenience, pet owners can register online for grooming or clinic services and access information about their pets through our customer portal. Whether your pet needs a refreshing grooming session or veterinary attention, Bethlehem is here to support their care.
